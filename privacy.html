Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

OMECOnnect is the customer and staff web app of OMECO (Occidental Mindoro Electric Cooperative). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form). If you use Continue with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or other Google data.
  • Profile: full name, mobile number, municipality and barangay of your service location.
  • OMECO account: your OMECO account number and verification requests, and the bills, due dates and payment status linked to it.
  • Concerns and messages: concerns or complaints you submit (with any photos you attach), the status updates on them, the star rating you give after a concern is resolved, and questions you send to the in-app assistant.
  • Photos: an optional profile photo (or your Google profile photo if you sign in with Google), photos attached to concerns, and meter photos you send with a meter reading. Photos are shrunk on your phone before upload, which also removes hidden data such as the phone's location.
  • Meter readings you submit and staff's decision on them.
  • Payments: when you pay online, the amount, method and reference number. Card and e-wallet details are entered on PayMongo's page and are never seen or stored by OMECOnnect.
  • Phone notifications: if you allow them, a technical address your phone's browser gives us to deliver alerts.
  • Security records: sign-in events, two-step verification settings and staff actions (audit logs).

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To link your OMECO electric service account and show your bills, announcements and notifications.
  • To receive, route and resolve your concerns and service requests.
  • To let authorized OMECO staff verify accounts and manage consumer records for their service areas.
  • To remind you of due bills, show the power status of your barangay, and group outage reports by area.
  • To measure service quality (for example, how fast concerns are resolved and your ratings), in summary form only.

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use tracking or advertising cookies.

Who processes it

  • Supabase – stores the database and handles sign-in (servers in Singapore).
  • Vercel – hosts the web pages.
  • Google – only when you choose Continue with Google.
  • PayMongo – processes online payments, only when you choose Pay now.
  • Anthropic (Claude) – when the AI assistant is switched on, your question and the records needed to answer it (your bills, concerns and area advisories) are sent to generate the answer. They are not used to train AI models.

Access to records is restricted by database rules: customers can see only their own data, and only authorized OMECO staff accounts can open the admin dashboard.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as OMECO needs it for service, billing and legal record-keeping. A protected backup copy of the records is made every night and kept for 14 days, then deleted automatically.

If you delete your OMECOnnect account, your sign-in, profile, photos, notifications and assistant chats are deleted. OMECO's own billing records for your electric service (accounts, bills, payments) are kept as required for billing and audit, but are no longer linked to the app. Concerns you sent are kept without your name.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or object to its processing. In the app's Profile page you can, at any time:

  • update your name, location, photo and linked accounts;
  • Download my data – get a file with everything OMECOnnect stores about you;
  • Delete my account – remove your OMECOnnect account (see above for what OMECO keeps).

For other requests, send a concern through the app or contact OMECO at any of its business offices. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Changes
